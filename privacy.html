Privacy Policy, EWK uploader

EWK uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of Everything Worth Knowing. It uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

It only reads the basic account details needed to confirm the right account (name and username).

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are sent only to the platforms' official APIs.

The tool collects no data about viewers or anyone else, shows no ads, uses no analytics, and shares or sells nothing.

The owner can revoke access at any time in each platform's settings.

To delete everything the tool stores, the owner deletes the token files on their computer and removes the app's access in each platform's settings.

Contact: everythingworthknowingtv@gmail.com
